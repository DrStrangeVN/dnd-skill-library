# Narrative Arc — Beeknoee deep-dive structure

Distilled from 3 analyzed videos (Agent Reach, Impeccable, Brigade).
Adapt beats to the topic; keep the tension curve.

1. **Hook** (0-10s): big claim or provocative question as hero type.
   ("93.000 sao GitHub: repo cho AI đọc cả Internet miễn phí… nhưng cái giá")
2. **Pain** (10-25s): the problem it solves, visceral and concrete.
   ("AI của bạn đang mù", "Web trông như AI làm?")
3. **The fix** (25-45s): what the thing does, in 1 line + demo visual.
   ("Thuốc chữa: 1 dòng", "Vũ khí #1: từ vựng")
4. **Data proof** (45-75s): stars, numbers, charts, terminal evidence.
   Count-ups, stat pills, star-history charts.
5. **Community hype** (75-100s): "Cộng đồng phát cuồng", quotes, HN/tweets.
6. **Hidden cost** (100-125s): the twist — cookie/APi bills, "Cái giá phải trả",
   "gần như không làm gì", warning stamps.
7. **Clash** (125-150s): two sides — "Dân dev chia hai phe", PHE 1 vs PHE 2 cards.
8. **Verdict** (150-end): answer the hook question
   ("Sư tử thật hay gầm to?"), 3 takeaway chips, repo/source CTA.

Rules:
- Every beat gets its own scene(s) with a pill title.
- Numbers on screen must match the source (README, GitHub API).
- No invented performance/speed claims.
- End with where to find the thing (repo URL), not a platform promo.

# Tutorial arc (variant A — Cường it style, 30-45s)

1. **Hook** (0-5s): result promise as hero type. ("Gõ 1 chủ đề, AI vẽ tay cả video")
2. **Steps** (5-25s): numbered cards 01/02/03, one idea each, fast cuts.
3. **Demo/proof** (25-32s): browser mockup or before/after.
4. **Checklist recap** (32-37s): animated checkmarks, 3 rows max.
5. **CTA** (37-40s): Follow/Like/Save row + repo URL + channel header.
