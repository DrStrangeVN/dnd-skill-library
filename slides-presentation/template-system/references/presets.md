# Presentation Pro Presets

These are default visual directions. A user-supplied template or brand guideline overrides them.

## 1. Executive Light
Best for: BOD reports, management updates, financial and operating reviews.
- Bright background, restrained accent, high data density.
- Strong conclusion titles.
- KPI cards, clean bars/lines, compact tables.
- Tone: precise, calm, decision-oriented.

## 2. Executive Dark
Best for: keynote-style leadership updates, premium internal reviews, high-contrast screens.
- Dark canvas, high-contrast type, one vivid accent.
- Fewer elements per slide than Executive Light.
- Tone: premium, confident, concise.

## 3. Business Proposal
Best for: client/partner proposals and service pitches.
- Spacious layout, strong section breaks, benefit-led copy.
- Diagrams, process, timeline, scope matrix and commercial summary.
- Tone: professional, persuasive, practical.

## 4. Sports Sponsorship
Best for: teams, leagues, tournaments, athletes and sports properties.
- Image-forward storytelling with disciplined commercial slides.
- Audience, reach, rights inventory, activation concepts, packages, measurement.
- Tone: energetic but commercially credible.

## 5. Event Experience
Best for: activations, launches, festivals, conferences and brand events.
- Concept-first visuals, journey maps, zones, run-of-show, timeline.
- Tone: experiential, visual, execution-aware.

## 6. Investor / Strategic Pitch
Best for: fundraising, project investment and internal business cases.
- Minimal styling with clear evidence hierarchy.
- Market, model, traction, economics, ask and milestones.
- Tone: direct, evidence-led, compact.

## Selection fallback
If no preset clearly fits, use Executive Light for reports and Business Proposal for proposals. State the proposed direction only when the user asks about style; otherwise proceed without unnecessary confirmation.
