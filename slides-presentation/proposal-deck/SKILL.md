# Proposal Deck

Create a proposal that makes the opportunity, value, plan, proof, scope and next step easy to understand.

## Workflow
1. Identify recipient, objective, problem/opportunity, proposed solution, scope, budget if provided, and desired call-to-action.
2. Build a narrative from recipient need to proposed value.
3. Lead with relevance, not company history.
4. Translate features into recipient benefits and outcomes.
5. Make deliverables concrete and separate optional items from committed scope.
6. Use timelines, process diagrams, packages, and deliverable matrices when useful.
7. End with a clear next step.

## Default structure
Cover; Opportunity/context; Objective; Audience/challenge; Proposed concept; Strategic approach; Key deliverables; Implementation plan; Timeline; Investment/commercial terms; Proof; Next step.

## Rules
Premium, confident, concise. Avoid generic marketing filler. Preserve facts and clearly label assumptions.
