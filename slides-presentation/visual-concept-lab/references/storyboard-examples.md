# Storyboard example — F&B partnership

Use only when appropriate to the brief; do not force this structure on every sponsor.

1. **Cover — The energy behind the game.** One strong documentary or approved team image; concise proposition, sponsor, season, and draft status.
2. **Why this team, why now.** A verified club fact plus current-season context and a sponsor-relevant audience need. Cite dates and sources.
3. **The creative platform.** One memorable thought with a hero image and a real fan/team behavior.
4. **From prep to buzzer.** A 3–4 frame storyboard showing the idea in team routine, content, game day, and fan reward. Label concepts as proposed.
5. **Rights and delivery.** A compact matrix of approved or proposed deliverables, counts, channel, owner, timing, proof, and dependencies.
6. **Investment and measurement.** Price structure, term, cash/in-kind split, VAT status, confirmed value, and a KPI plan with baseline/target/source clearly separated.
7. **Decision and next step.** Exact approvals and information needed to move to contracting.

Reduce to four slides only for a teaser. Keep the evidence, rights, and commercial caveats visible even in a short version.
