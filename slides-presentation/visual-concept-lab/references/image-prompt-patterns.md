# Image prompt patterns

## Generic conceptual hero

“Create a cinematic, editorial 16:9 conceptual image for a Vietnamese professional basketball partnership proposal. Show [specific non-identifiable subject/action] at [setting]. Use warm translucent apricot-orange reflections and soft glass-like highlights against a clean warm-ivory and ink-navy environment. Composition: [camera/viewpoint], strong focal point on [object], useful negative space at [side] for PowerPoint headline. The image is aspirational concept art, not documentary photography. No readable text, no logos, no jersey numbers, no identifiable real athlete, no fake sponsor product, no watermark.”

## Food ritual storyboard

Create separate images rather than one collage: (1) careful food preparation before practice, (2) an anonymous team table after training, (3) a game-day sampling or fan-reward concept, (4) close-up of a meal beside basketball texture. Do not depict an actual Danang Dragons event or player without approved reference photography.

## Native slide assets

Use supplied team photos for real roster/venue moments. Use generated art as a background or concept image only, then add all logos, labels, QR placeholders, numbers, and sponsor products as editable native PowerPoint elements. Any QR code shown in a concept must be clearly marked as a placeholder and must not resolve to a real destination.
