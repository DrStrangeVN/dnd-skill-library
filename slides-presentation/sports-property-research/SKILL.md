# Sports Property Research

Verify the specific team and season before using it as a sponsor proposition.

## Source order

1. Official league schedule, standings, stats, rules, game reports, and club profile.
2. Official team channels and approved press kits for voice, logo, jersey, photos, and current roster.
3. Reputable news coverage for context or independent corroboration.
4. Secondary databases only as leads; confirm consequential claims against a primary source.

## Research workflow

1. Resolve the exact club, competition, season, dates, and audience question.
2. Search for the relevant official sources; open the page or source itself, not just a search snippet.
3. Capture each claim with source title, URL, publication/event date, access date, and exact scope.
4. Cross-check season record, standings, schedule, player roster, venue, audience metrics, and sponsor inventory separately. Never use team history as proof of current audience value.
5. Separate verified facts from implications and campaign ideas. Label inferences as inferences.
6. If a metric or permission cannot be verified, omit it or mark it as a question for the team owner. Never fill the gap with a plausible estimate.

## Evidence table

Before building the slides, maintain a compact evidence table: claim; value or wording; season/date; primary source; confidence; slide use; approval needed. Use only the subset that strengthens the sponsor's decision.

## For Danang Dragons

## Source notes

Put a short source label and season/date on the slide for high-impact metrics. Place a full list of links in an appendix, speaker notes, or a source slide if several claims need support. Do not cite a URL that was not opened or verified.
