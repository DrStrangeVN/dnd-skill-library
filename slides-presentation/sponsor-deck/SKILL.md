# Sponsor Deck

Translate an event, team, property, community or media platform into a clear sponsorship value proposition.

## Workflow
1. Identify property, sponsor target, audience, rights inventory, activation opportunities, evidence, commercial terms, and desired outcome.
2. Tailor the deck to the sponsor category and business objective.
3. Separate guaranteed rights from optional activation ideas.
4. Quantify reach, attendance, impressions, content, hospitality, leads, or community impact only when supported.
5. Present packages clearly.
6. Include measurement and post-campaign reporting.

## Default structure
Cover; Partnership opportunity; Property/team/event overview; Audience profile; Reach & engagement; Partnership fit; Sponsorship assets; Activation concepts; Media/content plan; Hospitality/community benefits; Measurement; Packages/investment; Timeline; Next step.

## Rules
Do not invent metrics. Show sponsor benefit, not only logo placement. Make rights, deliverables, evidence, owner and timing easy to track.
