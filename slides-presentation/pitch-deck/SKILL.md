# Pitch Deck

Explain the opportunity, solution, evidence, model and ask in a compact persuasive narrative.

## Workflow
1. Identify the audience and exact ask.
2. Define problem/opportunity and why it matters now.
3. Explain solution simply before detail.
4. Show evidence: traction, market data, economics, proof points or operational readiness when available.
5. Distinguish actual performance from forecasts.
6. State assumptions behind forecasts.
7. End with ask, use of funds/resources and milestones.

## Default structure
Cover; Problem/opportunity; Solution; Why now; Market/audience; Product/offering; Business model; Traction/proof; Go-to-market; Competitive context; Financial outlook; Team; Ask/use of funds; Milestones.

## Rules
Never fabricate market size, traction, valuation or financial data. Keep forecast and actual data visually distinct.
