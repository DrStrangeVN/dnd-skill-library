# Executive Summary

Synthesize the material for a decision-maker who may read only these slides.

## Workflow

1. Identify the decision, reporting period and audience.
2. Extract only material outcomes, drivers, risks, opportunities and actions.
3. Prioritize what changed and why it matters.
4. Quantify takeaways when the source supports it.
5. Distinguish facts from interpretation and recommendation.
6. End with decisions required or next actions when relevant.

## Default 2-slide pattern

### Slide 1 — Executive Snapshot
- 3-5 key takeaways
- KPI scorecard or headline metrics
- One sentence on overall status

### Slide 2 — What needs attention
- Key drivers
- Risks / blockers
- Opportunities
- Decisions / next actions

Use one slide when the material is simple; use three when financial or operational detail needs its own page.
