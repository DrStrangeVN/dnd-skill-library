# Vietnam legal source map — starting references

Checked 23 September 2026. This list is not exhaustive and does not establish which law applies to a particular agreement. Verify the current version, status, amendments, implementing rules, and effective date for every task.

## Official databases and publication sources

- National Database of Legal Normative Documents: https://vbpl.vn/
- Government Legal Document Portal: https://vanban.chinhphu.vn/
- Official Gazette: https://congbao.chinhphu.vn/
- National Legal Portal: https://phapluat.gov.vn/

## Common starting statutes

- **Civil Code 91/2015/QH13** — official Government portal text: https://vanban.chinhphu.vn/default.aspx?docid=183188&pageid=27160. Check the official metadata and amendments before relying on a provision.
- **Commercial Law 36/2005/QH11** — an official consolidated text, 113/VBHN-VPQH dated 27 August 2025, is listed at https://vanban.chinhphu.vn/?classid=2629&docid=215232&pageid=27160. Verify whether later legislation has changed it since consolidation.
- **Law on Electronic Transactions 20/2023/QH15** — official legal database metadata/text route: https://vbpl.vn/TW/Pages/vbpq-thuoctinh.aspx?ItemID=165913. The law was issued on 22 June 2023; check effective date and the contract type's specific signature/form requirements.
- **Law on Protection of Consumers' Rights 19/2023/QH15** — official legal database source: https://vbpl.vn/ninhthuan/Pages/vbpq-van-ban-goc.aspx?ItemID=161263. The database lists an effective date of 1 July 2024; verify related regulations and any later changes.
- **Law on Personal Data Protection 91/2025/QH15** — official legal database metadata/text route: https://vbpl.vn/TW/Pages/vbpq-thuoctinh.aspx?ItemID=179252. The database lists an effective date of 1 January 2026; check implementing instruments and scope for the data processing at issue.

## Select law by contract type

Depending on the agreement, search for current official instruments on employment, housing/land, construction, investment, enterprise authority, intellectual property, credit/security, insurance, tax, e-commerce, competition, public procurement, technology transfer, personal data, consumer protection, or sector-specific licensing. Do not infer applicability from the contract title alone.

If legal sources cannot be opened or reconciled, do not convert search-result snippets into a definitive conclusion. Report the exact gap and recommend Vietnamese counsel review.
