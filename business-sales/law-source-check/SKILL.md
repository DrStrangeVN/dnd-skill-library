# Law Source Check

Legal rules change and depend on contract type, parties, dates, subject matter, sector, and governing law. Do not rely on memory for current legal conclusions.

## Source hierarchy

1. Official National Database of Legal Normative Documents (`vbpl.vn`).
2. Government Legal Document Portal and official consolidated texts (`vanban.chinhphu.vn`).
3. Official Gazette (`congbao.chinhphu.vn`).
4. Competent ministry, regulator, or court materials for sectoral rules and implementation.
5. Reputable legal commentary only to locate or interpret an issue; it does not replace primary law.

## Workflow

1. Define the legal question and time: date signed, date performed, and date the user wants the law assessed as of.
2. Confirm governing law and whether mandatory rules, foreign law, treaty, local authority, arbitration rule, or a sectoral regime may apply.
3. Search for the named act and related regulations. Open primary sources and inspect the legal text/metadata.
4. Verify document number, issuing body, promulgation and effective dates, current status, amendments/consolidation, replacement, and transitional rules. Distinguish an original law from a consolidated text and from an amending instrument.
5. Read the exact article/paragraph and surrounding provisions. Confirm that it applies to this contract type and fact pattern.
6. Cite title, document number, article/paragraph, official URL, status/effective date, and access date. Quote only the short text needed.
7. Explain what the source clearly says, what is an interpretation, and what remains uncertain. If the source is inaccessible or conflict is unresolved, say so and recommend lawyer verification.

## Privacy when searching

Never place contract text, party names, personal identifiers, confidential amounts, account numbers, or sensitive facts in a web query. Convert the issue into an abstract question, for example: “Vietnam commercial contract late-payment interest applicable rules as of [date]”.
