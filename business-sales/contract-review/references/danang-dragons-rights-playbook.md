# Danang Dragons rights-first contract review checklist

Use this as a prompt list, not a legal conclusion or a fixed set of approved company positions. Tailor it to the contract and current league, venue, player, and company rules.

## Brand, content, and sports rights

- Precisely define use of company name, team name, trademarks, logo, uniform, footage, event photos, and player/staff names or likenesses.
- Limit media, territory, duration, purpose, editing, reposting, sublicensing, paid media, AI training, and post-termination use.
- Require approval before publication; set a review window and a correction/removal process.
- Confirm whether VBA, a venue, a player, a photographer, a broadcaster, or another partner owns or controls each right. Do not grant rights DND may not own.
- Define category exclusivity narrowly by product/service and duration, with existing sponsor conflicts and carve-outs listed.
- Control product claims, endorsements, use of player statements, uniforms, and appearance commitments.

## Commercial delivery

- Specify deliverable, quantity, date, channel, owner, proof of completion, acceptance, make-good, and dependency for each benefit.
- For in-kind support, define product, quality, quantity, delivery window, storage, acceptance, substitutions, and what happens if delivery fails.
- Identify cash/in-kind split, currency, price, tax/VAT treatment, invoices, payment milestones, late payment, and any conditions to payment.
- Define change requests, approval workflow, postponement, cancellation, force majeure, refunds/credit, and rescheduling.

## Exposure and remedies

- Check liability cap basis, exclusions, indemnities, insurance, third-party claims, data/IP breach, confidentiality, and remedies for each side.
- Review termination triggers, cure period, immediate suspension rights, outstanding payments, return/deletion of materials, and continuing brand use.
- Review confidentiality and personal-data clauses for purpose, access, security, retention, breach notice, sub-processors, and deletion.
- Verify the signatory's authority and any company/internal approvals for scope, amount, term, or exclusivity.

For player employment, medical data, image rights, licensing, regulated products, large sponsorship value, financing, property, disputes, or cross-border terms, flag for Vietnamese counsel and relevant league/club decision-maker review.
