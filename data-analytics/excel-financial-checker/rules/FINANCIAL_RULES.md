# Financial Review Rules

## Budget
- Reconcile Budget / Actual / Forecast.
- Calculate/check variance and variance % where already part of the model.
- Detect duplicate cost centers/categories and missing mapping.
- Check summary totals against detail.

## Payroll
- Verify employee IDs/names for duplicates.
- Trace gross-to-net logic.
- Reconcile departmental and overall totals.
- Highlight unusual bonus/OT/deduction changes when comparative periods exist.
- Treat legal/tax/insurance compliance as `Needs Review` unless explicit rules are provided.

## Cash Flow
- Opening + Inflow - Outflow = Closing.
- Prior closing to next opening continuity.
- Reconcile bank/cash schedules where present.
- Check signs and classification consistency.

## Financial-model formatting
When building or materially restyling and no house style conflicts:
- zeros: `-`;
- negatives: red + parentheses;
- inputs: blue font;
- formulas: black font;
- internal sheet links: green font;
- key assumptions needing update: yellow fill.
